How the engine moves

Crankshaft: converts piston travel into rotation. Connecting rods: transfer force between pistons and crankshaft. Pistons: move up and down inside the cylinder sleeves.

Camshafts: rotate at half crankshaft speed in a four-stroke engine. Intake and exhaust valves: open and close in sequence to control gas exchange.

The animation uses a generic slider-crank and simplified valve timing for teaching. It is not OEM timing data, a validated Honda J35A6 model, or a substitute for service specifications.

Smog / emissions basics
  • Readiness monitors (misfire, fuel system, components, catalyst, EVAP, O2 sensor and heater, EGR) must report “complete” before most smog tests.
  • Clearing codes resets monitors. A drive cycle is needed afterwards, so never clear codes just before a test.
  • Misfire or rich/lean faults damage the catalyst; fix them before blaming a P0420.
  • EVAP leaks (e.g. loose cap) cause failures with no driveability symptom.
  • Permanent codes (Mode 0A) clear only when the ECU’s own test passes, not by erasing codes.
  • Test rules vary by state/country: follow your local program.
